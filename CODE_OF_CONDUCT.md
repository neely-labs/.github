# Code of Conduct

Neely Labs welcomes thoughtful participation from people of all backgrounds.

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Discuss ideas and behavior rather
than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, and disclosure of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to protect a healthy project environment. Enforcement decisions
will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact the repository maintainers through
an appropriate private channel. For security vulnerabilities, use the
repository's private vulnerability-reporting flow.
